# WA Sender Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

WA Sender is a Chrome extension that fills a message template into a chat in
WhatsApp Web, so you can review the message and send it yourself.

## What the extension handles

- **Phone numbers and field values** (for example a customer's name or street)
  that you type into the panel. They are used only inside your WhatsApp Web
  page to open the chat and fill in the message. They are not stored, and are
  not sent to the developer or anyone else.
- **Your message template and field names**, which you set on the settings page.
  They are saved with Chrome's storage, and if Chrome sync is turned on, Chrome
  syncs them to your other computers through your Google account. The developer
  has no access to them.
- **The panel button's position** on the page, saved in your browser.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.
- It has no analytics, tracking or ads, and does not contact any server of its own.
- It does not read your WhatsApp messages or contacts.
- It never sends a WhatsApp message by itself. You always press send.
- It runs only on web.whatsapp.com.

When you open a chat, WhatsApp Web itself checks the phone number with
WhatsApp's servers, just as when you search for a number in WhatsApp yourself.
That is covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

## Contact

For questions, use the support tab on WA Sender's Chrome Web Store page.
